With 53 days left before the deadline, the DGFiP and AIFE have published a detailed status update: the **State's infrastructure is ready** for September 1, 2026. Here's a tour of the official figures and milestones.

## The technical building blocks are in place

Rollout has been gradual and is now operational:

- **Business directory** in production, enhanced with bulk lookup (up to 5,000 SIREN at once);
- **Declaration service** (pilot) in qualification since October 2025, then in real production since February 2026;
- **Performance tests** on volume carried out successfully: the infrastructure can absorb the full expected traffic;
- **Public sector**: Chorus Pro as the reference platform, with the technical chain tested and validated.

## A fast-growing ecosystem

On the market side, momentum is clear:

- **138 Certified Platforms** registered (+30% versus January 2026), 15 in testing, 7 being registered;
- **2 million companies** already have a receiving address in the directory;
- **40 Certified Platforms** have already transmitted flows from 10,000 issuing companies to 12,000 receiving ones;
- **95 Peppol contracts** signed, for near-complete coverage — France has become the number one country by number of registered addresses.

## The legal and regulatory framework is being finalised

- The **decree** and **implementing order** are expected before the end of summer 2026, followed by the publication of the doctrine;
- The **3 AFNOR standards** were updated (June 30 – July 1), incorporating new use cases (bidirectional self-billing) and sector-specific chapters (energy, media, real estate).

> Worth remembering: standardisation is a **living** process, meant to evolve with practice. Regulatory monitoring is an integral part of any e-invoicing project.
