The latest e-invoicing barometer, carried out with IPSOS among small and mid-sized businesses, paints a precise picture of how prepared companies are. Good news: momentum is accelerating.

## Confidence is rising

Business owners are approaching the deadline with growing optimism:

- **69%** have already chosen their solution or are in the process of choosing it;
- **76%** say they're confident in their ability to be ready on time;
- that figure climbs to **88%** among those who have already chosen their solution.

The message is clear: **taking action eases the anxiety**. Choosing a platform is the first reassuring step.

## Concerns are shifting

As companies move into action, worries are evolving:

- **cost** drops 11 points among concerns, even though it remains central;
- **data security** rises 11 points and becomes the **number one concern**.

## Security: an issue not to overlook

This rise in security concerns is legitimate — and, somewhat paradoxically, the reform partly addresses it. By structuring exchanges through **certified operators** and identification mechanisms (KYC/KYB), it acts as a security lever, at a time when **91% of hacks** originate from email.

> Worth remembering: security shouldn't be a barrier, but a criterion for choosing your platform and a component of your project in its own right.
